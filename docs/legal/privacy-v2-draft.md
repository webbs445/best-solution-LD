# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

## 1. Who we are

This policy covers the website business.best-solution.ae. The site is run by Best Solution, a private business consultancy in Business Bay, Dubai ("Best Solution", "we", "us"). We are not a government entity.

If you have a question about your personal data, email connect@best-solution.ae.

## 2. What we collect

When you fill in a consultation or callback form on this site, we collect:

- your name, phone number and email address;
- the topic of your enquiry and any message you write;
- any short answers you give about your plans in the form (for example your type of business or the support you need).

When you arrive from an online ad, the link can carry a click ID (gclid, gbraid or wbraid) and campaign values (UTM tags). If you send a form, we keep these with your enquiry so we know which ad it came from.

Only if you accept cookies, analytics and advertising tags from Google Analytics 4, Google Ads, Microsoft Clarity and Meta record how the site is used and whether a visit came from an ad. See section 3.

We do not ask for identity papers, payment card details or other sensitive data on this site.

## 3. Cookies and your consent

When you first visit, a cookie banner asks for your choice: Reject all, Accept all, or Manage preferences (Analytics and Marketing). Until you choose, no analytics or advertising tag runs. We use Google Consent Mode so Google's tags follow your choice.

We store your choice on your device so we do not ask again. You can change it at any time with the Cookie Settings link at the bottom of every page.

## 4. Why we use your data

- To reply to your enquiry and arrange your consultation, by phone, WhatsApp or email. The basis is your request.
- To measure our advertising: which ads lead to enquiries. This includes sending the click ID, the time of your enquiry and our enquiry reference back to Google Ads. The basis is your consent where cookies are involved, and our interest in knowing which ads work.
- To keep the site secure and working.

We do not sell your personal data.

## 5. Who we share it with

- Our customer records system (ERPNext) and our internal lead sheet (Google Sheets), used only by our team to handle enquiries.
- Google (Analytics and Ads), Microsoft (Clarity) and Meta, only after your consent and only as described above.
- Our website hosting provider (Vercel), which processes requests to run the site.
- Authorities, only where UAE law requires it.

Some of these providers may store data outside the UAE. We only use providers that protect personal data under their own published terms.

## 6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as we need them to reply to you and follow up, and after that only as long as UAE law requires. Analytics data is kept for the period set in each tool.

## 7. Your rights

Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) you can ask us to:

- give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
- correct data that is wrong;
- delete your data;
- stop using your data where you gave consent (withdrawing consent does not affect use before you withdrew it).

Email connect@best-solution.ae. We may ask you to confirm who you are before we act. We aim to reply within 30 days.

## 8. Security

Only staff who handle enquiries can see your details. The site uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

## 9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
